Coconut Chutney

Fresh coconut chutney with roasted gram, green chilli and a simple tempering.

⏱ 10 minutes · South Indian kitchen favourite

Ingredients

  • 200 g fresh coconut
  • 50 g roasted gram
  • 3 green chillies
  • 10 g ginger
  • 80 ml yogurt or water
  • 4 g salt

How to make Coconut Chutney

  1. Add coconut, roasted gram, green chillies, ginger and salt to a mixer jar.
  2. Add yogurt or water gradually and grind to a smooth or slightly coarse chutney.
  3. Adjust thickness and seasoning to taste.
  4. Serve fresh. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and use a clean spoon.
