Curry Leaves Podi

A fragrant curry-leaf spice powder for rice, idli and dosa.

⏱ 25 minutes · South Indian kitchen favourite

Ingredients

  • 180 g dried curry leaves
  • 100 g urad dal
  • 80 g chana dal
  • 70 g dry red chillies
  • 40 g sesame seeds
  • 30 g salt and spices

How to make Curry Leaves Podi

  1. Wash curry leaves and dry them completely; moisture can shorten storage life.
  2. Dry-roast the curry leaves on low heat until crisp. Roast dals, chillies and sesame separately.
  3. Cool all ingredients fully, then grind to a powder.
  4. Mix in salt and store airtight only after the powder has cooled completely.
