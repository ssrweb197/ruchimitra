Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about RuchiMitra recipes, ingredient quantities, kitchen calculators and everyday cooking. Always use your own judgement and follow safe food-handling practices.

1. What can I find on RuchiMitra?

RuchiMitra brings together South Indian home-cooking recipes, podi and chutney ideas, pickle recipes, kitchen tips, cooking guides and tools for estimating ingredient quantities and recipe costs.

2. Are the recipe quantities suitable for every kitchen?

Recipe quantities are starting points. Ingredient size, moisture, brand, grind, heat level and personal taste can change the result. Read the full recipe and adjust seasoning carefully to suit your household.

3. How should I scale a recipe for more or fewer servings?

Use the serving or quantity calculator where available as a planning aid. Multiplying every ingredient by the same factor may not work perfectly for salt, chilli, souring agents, water or cooking time. Adjust these gradually and taste as you cook.

4. Can I use the ingredient calculator as an exact shopping estimate?

No. It provides an estimate based on the inputs and assumptions shown. Actual purchase quantities can vary with cleaning, trimming, peeling, cooking loss, pack sizes and ingredient availability.

5. How do I estimate the cost of a recipe?

Enter the ingredient quantities and the prices you actually pay, using consistent units. The result is an approximate ingredient cost; it may not include fuel, labour, packaging, wastage or overheads unless the tool explicitly asks for them.

6. How can I get a better flavour when roasting spices?

Follow the relevant recipe or guide and roast over controlled heat, stirring frequently. Different spices toast at different speeds, so avoid relying on one fixed time for every ingredient. Cool roasted spices before grinding or storing.

7. How should I store podi and spice powders?

Let the powder cool completely, then keep it in a clean, dry, airtight container away from moisture, heat and direct sunlight. Use a dry spoon and check for any change in smell, appearance or signs of spoilage before use.

8. What should I know before making pickles?

Follow a tested recipe with its stated ingredient proportions and preparation method. Keep utensils and containers clean and dry, use the specified salt, oil and acidity, and follow safe storage guidance. Do not assume a homemade pickle is shelf-stable without a validated process.

9. Can I change chilli, salt or sourness in a recipe?

You can adapt flavour to your preference, but make small changes and taste as you go. In pickling and preservation recipes, do not casually reduce salt, oil or acidic ingredients because they can be important to the preservation method.

10. What if I find an error or have a recipe suggestion?

Please use the Contact Us page to share the recipe or tool name, the page link and a clear description of the issue or suggestion. This helps us review it accurately.

Food safety note: RuchiMitra provides general home-cooking information, not personalised dietary, allergy or food-safety certification. Check ingredient labels, prevent cross-contamination, cook foods thoroughly and follow reliable food-preservation guidance.
