About RuchiMitra

Every recipe. Right quantity. Restaurant-style inspiration for everyday home kitchens.

RuchiMitra is a growing recipe companion created to make South Indian home cooking easier to explore and plan. We share ideas for podi, chutneys, spice mixes and pickles, along with a simple quantity calculator that helps adjust ingredient amounts.

What you can find here

  • South Indian-inspired recipes and kitchen ideas.
  • Ingredient quantity calculations for different batch sizes.
  • Recipe information in English and Telugu where available.
  • Practical inspiration for home cooks.

Our approach

We are building the collection gradually. Some recipe quantities and instructions are starter examples and may not have been professionally kitchen-tested. Please use your judgement, adjust seasoning to taste and follow trusted food-safety guidance, especially for pickles and preserved foods.

Thank you for visiting RuchiMitra and being part of our growing kitchen community.
