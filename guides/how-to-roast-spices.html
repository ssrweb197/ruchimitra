How to Roast Spices for Better Flavour

A little care during roasting can make podi, chutney powders and masalas more aromatic. The key is steady heat, small batches and patience.

Start with a dry pan

Use a clean, dry, heavy pan. Warm it over low to medium-low heat. Avoid adding wet ingredients to a dry-roasting step.

Roast ingredients separately

Different ingredients heat at different speeds. Roast dals, seeds, chillies and whole spices separately where practical, stirring often so they colour evenly.

Watch for aroma, not smoke

Stop when the ingredient smells fragrant and looks lightly toasted. If it smokes, darkens quickly or smells acrid, the heat is too high.

Cool before grinding

Spread roasted ingredients on a clean plate and let them cool fully. Grinding while hot can create condensation and affect texture.

Store thoughtfully

Keep cooled powders in a clean, completely dry, airtight container. Use a dry spoon and make smaller batches if you use them slowly.

Cooking results vary with ingredient freshness, cookware, heat and personal taste. Use clean utensils and follow safe food-handling practices.
