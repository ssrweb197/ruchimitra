How to Balance Spice, Salt and Sourness

Taste changes as a dish cooks and rests. Make small adjustments, taste again, and remember that the right balance depends on the ingredients and the people eating.

If a dish is too spicy

Where suitable, increase the amount of the main unsalted ingredients or add a compatible mild component. Dairy may suit some dishes but not every chutney, podi or pickle.

If it tastes too salty

Increase the unsalted base ingredients when the recipe allows. Adding water may dilute a curry or gravy, but it can also change texture and flavour.

If it is too sour

Add more of the main ingredients or a small amount of a compatible balancing ingredient. Avoid adding lots of sugar as a universal fix.

Adjust gradually

Add a little at a time, mix thoroughly and taste again. Do not make several large corrections at once.

Write down what worked

Record ingredient changes and batch size. This makes it easier to repeat a successful version next time.

Cooking results vary with ingredient freshness, cookware, heat and personal taste. Use clean utensils and follow safe food-handling practices.
