How to Store Podi and Spice Powders

Homemade podi and spice powders stay at their best when moisture, heat and contamination are kept away. These practical habits help protect flavour.

Cool completely before packing

Never seal freshly roasted or ground ingredients while warm. Let them cool fully so trapped steam does not create moisture inside the jar.

Choose a clean, dry container

Use an airtight food-safe container. Make sure the jar and lid are completely dry before filling.

Keep moisture out

Always use a clean, dry spoon. Avoid sprinkling powder directly over a steaming pot or kettle.

Store away from heat and sunlight

A cool, dry cupboard away from the stove is usually a better place than a shelf beside the cooktop or a sunny window.

Check before use

Discard powders if you notice mould, damp clumps, insects or an unusual smell. Do not taste food that appears spoiled.

Cooking results vary with ingredient freshness, cookware, heat and personal taste. Use clean utensils and follow safe food-handling practices.
